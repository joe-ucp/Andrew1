1. **Availability.** Prove strict 25-bin readiness implies strict 4-bin readiness, with identical current-glucose gating. Hence long eligibility is contained in short eligibility and switch eligibility equals short eligibility; the common pre-event opportunity operator preserves both statements. Check the identities on DCLP3, REPLACE-BG and the original eligible CLVer intervals.
2. **Paired outcomes.** Retain the exact original event denominator and frozen M15/switch warning flags. Tabulate both, M15-only, switch-only and neither for all events and the M120-opportunity strata. Treat stratum differences as net changes, separately from gross discordances.
3. **Fixed-threshold policy diagnostics.** Replay M15 at .04 and .01; switching with M120 .04 and fallback M15 .04 or .01; and M120 alone at .04. Do not refit or retune. Compare the original switch against M15; the common-threshold switch against M15; the two switch fallback thresholds; the two standalone M15 thresholds; and standalone M120 against M15. Use 10,000 paired participant resamples with seed 20261023 for exploratory contrast intervals on the original held-out DCLP3 and REPLACE-BG samples.
4. **Conditional selection stability.** Use the complete per-person threshold tallies for all 10,772 settings and all 29 originally allocated validation participants, including zero-observation participants. Draw 1,000 participant resamples with seed 20261023; retain family, threshold and joint choices under the original warning-comparison and clinical-policy selection rules. Models remain fixed, no external data enter selection, and no retraining uncertainty is estimated.
5. **Descriptive context.** Summarize enrollment age, recorded gender/race/ethnicity, duration derived from reported diagnosis age, and local screening HbA1c from exactly the screening visit. Retain missingness and source provenance. Report observed/calendar coverage, event frequency, alarm categories, unknown-onset low runs and event-weighted versus person-level sensitivity. Full-cohort DCLP3 descriptions requested during revision are labeled separately from its held-out subset.
6. **Figure and interpretation.** Show paired discordance and the original frozen sensitivity/burden contrast intervals. Distinguish the original linear never-alarm sentinel if displayed. All new analyses are exploratory and conditional on fitted models. Equal numeric score thresholds do not equalize calibration or burden; shared refractory state couples branches. These policy contrasts do not identify a causal effect of history length, a clinical intervention effect or independent model-training uncertainty.
