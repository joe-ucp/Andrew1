This revision is prompted by review of the completed results; it is not a new confirmatory analysis or a rescue of the failed warning-burden transfer gate.

The exposure is index normalized two-hour MMTT C-peptide AUC, scaled per 0.20 nmol/L. Original pooled Poisson pseudo-maximum likelihood (PPML) adjustment comprises an intercept, exposure, target visits 39 and 52 (reference 26), lag centered at 60 days and scaled per 30 days, enrollment age centered at 12 years, intensive/Tandem and intensive/Medtronic management indicators (reference usual care), and cohort B (reference A). Offset is log observed-sensor days. Report child-cluster sandwich confidence intervals with finite-sample correction. These are conditional observational associations, not effects of increasing C-peptide.

## Sample and within-child questions

Construct exactly the original within-child sample by retaining every interval of each child with at least one unwarned event across the primary intervals. Assert agreement with the original fixed-child summary: 58 children, 164 intervals, 180 unwarned events. Refit the original pooled PPML on this exact sample. Report the original fixed-child estimate alongside it without overwriting that estimate. This separates change of sample from change of statistical estimand, while acknowledging that inclusion depends on observed outcomes.

For all primary children and the exact fixed-child sample, tabulate one, two and three available intervals; per-child AUC ranges; and counts with repeated observations and nonzero AUC variation (numerical tolerance 1e-10 nmol/L). Demean AUC within child, then residualize on within-child centered target-visit indicators; additionally residualize on target visit and lag. Report residual sum of squares, reduction from unadjusted within-child sum of squares, and children with nonzero residual variation. These are design-geometry diagnostics, not measures of power or proof of causal identification. Single-observation children have no within-child information.

Fit an exploratory correlated-effects (Mundlak) PPML in the full primary sample: decompose AUC into child mean and within-child deviation; retain original visit/lag terms and time-invariant covariates; augment with child means of the time-varying target-visit and lag terms. Record every column and rank. Omit only a nuisance column that is exactly constant with the intercept; if any remaining rank deficiency or separated mean exists, report non-estimability rather than select a favorable specification. Report both within- and between-child AUC coefficients per 0.20 nmol/L, clustered by child. A correlated-effects Poisson specification is not algebraically guaranteed to equal the original fixed-child model.

## Conditional warning probability

Use every observed qualifying event in the 276 primary intervals. Assert that ledger aggregation reproduces interval event, matched, unwarned and legal-opportunity counts exactly. Fit unpenalized event-level logistic regression of unwarned (1) versus usefully warned (0) on the same original pooled covariates. No offset applies to a binary conditional event outcome. Expand the small event ledger explicitly so the finite-sample cluster correction uses the actual number of events. Verify coefficient agreement with grouped two-count binomial likelihood at interval level, whose likelihood is identical up to constants. Report the conditional odds ratio per 0.20 nmol/L and child-cluster 95% interval, never call it a rate ratio. The outcome is conditional on occurrence and observation of an event, and selection into that event set can induce bias. Zero-event intervals make no contribution to this conditional likelihood; they remain in both original and revised rate models. This does not assess prevented events or actual symptoms/caregiver awareness. No future-data input is added to the policy.
